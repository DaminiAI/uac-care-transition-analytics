# UAC Care Transition Efficiency & Placement Outcome Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes operational efficiency within the UAC (Unaccompanied Alien Children) care transition pipeline managed by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

The system tracks children moving through multiple operational stages:

* CBP Apprehension & Custody
* Transfer to HHS Care
* Medical Screening & Case Management
* Sponsor Placement & Reunification

The project focuses on identifying:

* Transition efficiency
* Placement effectiveness
* Operational bottlenecks
* Backlog accumulation
* Outcome stability
* Future operational forecasting

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional monitoring systems focus mainly on aggregate custody counts but fail to measure operational process efficiency.

This project answers key operational questions:

* How efficiently are children transferred from CBP to HHS?
* Are sponsor discharges keeping pace with inflows?
* Where do operational bottlenecks emerge?
* Are placement outcomes improving or deteriorating over time?
